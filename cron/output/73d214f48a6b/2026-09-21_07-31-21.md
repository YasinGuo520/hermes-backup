## 📖 English Reading

Most new AI models try to talk to you, but a startup called TypeSafe AI has released one that refuses to write a single word. Its model, **Jev**, was launched in mid-September 2026 by Diogo Almeida, a former OpenAI researcher who helped build the training method behind ChatGPT. Instead of returning a paragraph, Jev takes a "state" — such as a support ticket or an invoice — plus a list of typed questions, and answers in about 0.1 to 0.5 seconds with probabilities attached. It offers just three kinds of answers: a Choice (pick one of up to 255 options), a Score (rate on a scale), and a "Noul" (a yes/no decision). TypeSafe charges $0.042 per million input tokens and nothing for output, and claims the model is roughly 200 times faster and 400 times cheaper than a frontier LLM — although those numbers come from its own tests, not independent benchmarks.
