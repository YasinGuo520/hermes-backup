## 📰 English Intro (3-5 sentences)

On August 24, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) cleared a new AI-powered software device called Tempus ECG-PH. The device analyzes standard 12-lead electrocardiograms, or ECGs, to detect signs of pulmonary hypertension, a condition that narrows the blood vessels in the lungs. Pulmonary hypertension is often underdiagnosed because its early symptoms are vague, and the standard diagnostic test is invasive. This is the third FDA-cleared cardiovascular device from Tempus AI, following its tools for detecting atrial fibrillation and low ejection fraction. The company hopes the software will help doctors identify at-risk patients much earlier.

## ✍️ 填空练习 (Fill in the blanks)

1. The FDA has ______ a new AI device that can detect signs of heart disease from a standard ECG.
2. Pulmonary hypertension is often ______ because its early symptoms are not specific.
3. Doctors hope the software will help them identify ______ patients much earlier.

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

1. **cleared**（批准、放行）— 注意这里用 clear 而不是 approve：FDA 语境里 "FDA clearance"（510(k) 通道）是固定说法。
2. **underdiagnosed**（被漏诊的）— 前缀 under- 表示"不足"，如 underpaid 报酬过低、undercooked 没煮熟。
3. **at-risk**（有风险的）— 连字符形容词修饰名词，写成 at risk patients 是错的。
