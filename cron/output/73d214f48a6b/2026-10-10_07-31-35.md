## 📖 English (原文链接: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/welcome-to-gemini-at-work-2026)

Google Cloud has just launched **Gemini Agent**, which it calls a "universal agent for work." Unlike a chatbot that only answers questions, it can plan a task and finish it on its own — for example, researching a market, building a spreadsheet, and turning the results into a slide deck. Google says you now "give it objectives, not instructions": you delegate an outcome and come back to finished work. The agent connects to Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Salesforce and any MCP server, and it can even run on a rival's model, including Anthropic's Claude. It is available as a limited preview for selected companies, and Google has not yet said when consumers will get it.
