### 📖 English Reading (≈5 sentences)

**Laya** is an open-source "decision engine" that answers *typed* questions — a choice, a score, or a simple yes/no — **instead of** writing paragraphs of text. You give it an email, a support ticket, or a JSON document, and it returns calibrated probabilities in **a single forward pass**, which takes about 33 milliseconds on a T4 GPU. Because it never generates sentences, there is nothing to parse and nothing to **hallucinate**. The project landed on GitHub on September 18 and has already passed **20,000 stars**, making it one of the fastest-growing AI repositories of the week. It arrives while everyone is arguing about **Jev**, a commercial "System 1" model that does something similar — so Laya markets itself as the free, local **alternative**.

### ✅ 参考答案

1. **instead of** — 注意后面必须接动名词 generating，不是 generate。
2. **pass** — "in a single forward pass" 是固定说法，指神经网络一次完整计算。
3. **hallucinate** — AI 领域的动词，指模型自信地编造不实信息。
4. **alternative** — 固定搭配：an alternative **to** something。

---

**今日语法点**：`instead of + V-ing` vs `instead + 句子`
- ✅ Laya answers questions *instead of* **generating** text.
- ✅ Laya doesn't generate text. *Instead*, it answers typed questions.
- ❌ instead of **generate** text
