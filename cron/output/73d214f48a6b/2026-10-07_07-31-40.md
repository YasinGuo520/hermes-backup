## 🇬🇧 English (原文精读)

Reflection AI, a New York-based startup backed by Nvidia, has unveiled **Beam**, its first open-weight AI model. Beam is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 501 billion total parameters and only 23 billion active per token. The company claims that Beam matches leading Chinese open models such as Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning benchmarks while using three to four times less inference compute. It was pretrained on 23.8 trillion tokens and supports a one-million-token context window, designed mainly for coding, reasoning, and agentic tasks. Reflection says it will release the model's weights under an Apache 2.0 license, along with a technical report, later this month.

**Source**: https://reflection.ai/blog/introducing-beam (TechCrunch report: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/reflection-debuts-beam-a-open-weight-ai-model-to-rival-chinese-models-at-lower-compute-cost/)
