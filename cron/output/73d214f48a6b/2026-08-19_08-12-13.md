## 📰 English Intro

Proton, the privacy-focused tech company, has launched a free tool called **AI Paper Trail** that shows users what AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude have learned about them from months or years of conversation history. After you download your chat history and upload it to the tool, it generates a personal report with an "AI Exposure Score" that measures how much personal information was extracted. The report reveals inferred details such as your occupation, habits, and relationships, and even estimates how much that data might be worth to an AI company. Proton says uploaded files are deleted immediately after analysis and are never stored on its servers. The tool was created to raise awareness of "shadow AI" — the growing problem of employees using personal AI chatbots for work without their company's oversight.
