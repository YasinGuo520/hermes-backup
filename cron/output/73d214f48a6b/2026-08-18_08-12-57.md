## ✍️ 填空练习 (Fill in the blanks)

1. Nvidia has agreed to ______ up to $105 billion in lease and power payments for the new data center.
2. The first 800 megawatts of computing ______ is expected to come online by 2028.
3. OpenAI will ______ the site from SB Energy for 20 years, and pay only as the capacity becomes available.

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

1. **guarantee**（担保）— guarantee payments = 担保付款，商业新闻高频动词；口语也可用 back（资金支持）
2. **capacity**（容量）— computing capacity = 算力，不可数名词，注意谓语用 is 不是 are
3. **lease**（租用）— lease A from B = 从B租A，介词用 from；名词形式 lease（租约）同形

</details>

**今天能记住的一个搭配**：**come online**（上线/投入使用）— "The first 800 megawatts are expected to come online by 2028." 数据中心、工厂、服务上线都用它，比 launch 更地道。
