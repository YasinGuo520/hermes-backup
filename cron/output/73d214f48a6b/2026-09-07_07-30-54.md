## 3️⃣ 填空练习（Fill in the blanks）

**语法点**: 现在完成时 / 被动语态 / 定语从句

1. OpenAI ______ (release) GPT-6 Astra this week, and many people ______ (already / test) its computer-use abilities.

2. Astra is the first OpenAI model that ______ (reach) the critical cybersecurity threshold, which means it can find security flaws ______ (help) by humans.

3. If you want to try it, you ______ (need) a paid plan — because the model costs 2.5 times ______ (much) than the previous one.

---

### ✅ 参考答案（先自己填，再对答案）

1. OpenAI **released** GPT-6 Astra this week, and many people **have already tested** its computer-use abilities.
   *（过去时间点 this week 用过去时；"已经测试过"强调结果用现在完成时。）*

2. Astra is the first OpenAI model that **has reached** the critical cybersecurity threshold, which means it can find security flaws **without being helped** by humans.
   *（先行词有 first 时定语从句常用完成时；"无需人工帮助"用 without being helped 被动。）*

3. If you want to try it, you **will need** a paid plan — because the model costs 2.5 times **as much as** the previous one.
   *（if 条件句主句用 will；"是……的 2.5 倍贵"用 times as much as。）*
