## ✍️ 填空练习 (Fill in the blanks)

1. OpenAI __________ (expand) its advertising program to 31 European markets on August 24.

2. Ads __________ (show) only to users on the free and Go tiers.

3. The ads do not influence the answer __________ you receive — the two are separate competitions.

---

<details>
<summary>👀 答案与解析</summary>

1. **expanded** — 过去时，讲过去发生的具体事件（on August 24）。
2. **are shown** — 被动语态：广告"被展示"给用户，主语 ads 是接受动作的一方。
3. **that** (或 which) — 关系代词，引导定语从句修饰 the answer；先行词是物，用 that/which。

</details>
