## Part 1 — English Reading (英文原文讲解)

> A code sleuth known as "pdfu" has uncovered private frameworks in iOS 27 and macOS Golden Gate that show Apple designed its new Siri architecture to work with third-party AI models on a surprisingly deep level. One mechanism, called **Model Delegation**, lets Claude appear inside Siri as an extension, just like the built-in ChatGPT extension. A second protocol goes even further: an inference provider in "Model Manager Services" can completely replace Apple's own server-side Siri model with another model, such as GPT-5.6 — meaning the outside model receives Siri's planner prompt and tool definitions, performs system actions, and hands the result back for Siri to present in its own voice. The EU's Digital Markets Act may have pushed Apple in this direction, since it requires giving third parties effective access to iOS features. For now, the feature is still hidden behind the surface and Apple has not yet opened the entitlement to developers.
