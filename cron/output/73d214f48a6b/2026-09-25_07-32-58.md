## 英文原文介绍（English）

Cisco Talos says it has found the first publicly documented Windows malware that lets artificial intelligence make its own tactical decisions. The sample, called **CLOSEDQUORUM**, does not wait for orders from a human operator or a command-and-control server. Instead, it asks up to four commercial language models — DeepSeek, Qwen, Mistral and Google Gemini — what to do next, then carries out whichever answer gets the most votes. Its menu of choices is fixed: steal credentials, inject code, establish persistence, or move to another machine. Talos has not confirmed that the malware was ever used against a real victim, and the public sample ships with placeholder API keys, so it is best read as a sign of where attacks are heading rather than an active campaign.

## 中文总结

思科 Talos 于 9 月 22 日披露一款名为 CLOSEDQUORUM 的 Windows 恶意软件：它不靠攻击者的命令服务器，而是把「下一步干什么」交给 DeepSeek、Qwen、Mistral、Gemini 四个大模型投票决定，票多的动作直接执行，票数打平时 DeepSeek 说了算。可选项只有四个——**窃取凭证 / 注入代码 / 持久化 / 横向移动**，判决策略还会通过 Discord webhook 回传给攻击者。

目前 Talos 尚未确认它被用于真实攻击（公开样本里是占位 API key、失效的 webhook），所以更该把它看成「信号」：攻击链中一段决策已经可以完全交给 AI，人只在事前搭管道。

## 填空练习（练语法/结构）

1. The malware does not wait for orders. ______ it lands on a machine, it asks four AI models what to do next.
   （用 **once** 引导时间状语从句）
2. Each model must answer in a fixed format; any answer that does not match ______ (throw) out.
3. The action that draws the most votes ______ (carry) out immediately.

## 答案

1. **Once** it lands on a machine, it asks... → *once* = "一……就"，比 when 更强调"立刻"
2. is **thrown** out → 被动语态：答案自己被系统"丢掉"（主语是 answer）
3. is **carried** out → 被动语态 + 一般现在时（any answer / the action 都是被执行的）
