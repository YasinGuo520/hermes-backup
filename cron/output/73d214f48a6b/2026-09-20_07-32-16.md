## 📖 English

Two years after leaving OpenAI, Diogo Almeida — one of the researchers behind ChatGPT — has released a new model called **Jev** through his startup, TypeSafe AI. Jev is built on the same transformer architecture as modern chatbots, but it is *not* a large language model: instead of writing sentences, it returns probabilities, which the company calls "calibrated decisions." The idea is to cut down on hallucinations, because the model must choose from a fixed set of pre-defined outputs rather than inventing free text. Developers say it is dramatically cheaper and faster — one engineer at Vercel reported results 5 to 18 times quicker and more accurate than OpenAI's model for checking whether a command is safe to run. TypeSafe describes Jev as a "System One" model focused on intuition rather than long chains of reasoning, and says it could also be used to monitor other AI agents or route tasks to the right model.
