### English

On October 15, 2026, Meta released the weights of **Muse Spark Open**, which it calls "the most capable US open-weight model to date." It is a natively multimodal, mixture-of-experts model with **816 billion total parameters, of which 42 billion are activated for each token**, and it supports a context window of up to **one million tokens**. Unlike Meta's earlier closed Muse Spark releases, this version can be downloaded from Hugging Face, Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden and Microsoft Foundry Models, and it works with common serving tools such as vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp and Unsloth. What makes the launch unusual is the hardware pitch: Meta says this is the first model in its class that can run entirely on your desktop, using **two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios** with MLX, which together provide a terabyte of unified memory. The catch is that those 512GB machines have not shipped yet and will cost tens of thousands of dollars — and the custom Muse Model License requires notification and a separate agreement if a model-serving business earns more than $20 million a year.
