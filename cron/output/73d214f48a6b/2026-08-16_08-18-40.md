## ✍️ 填空练习 (Fill in the blanks)

1. Elon Musk's SpaceX completed a $60 billion __________ of the AI coding tool Cursor.
2. Cursor says it will work ___ the company's Grok AI chatbot.
3. This deal shows how AI coding tools have become some of the most __________ software companies in the world.

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>

1. **acquisition**（收购）— 动词形式 acquire
2. **on** — work on sth = 从事/参与某事（注意介词，这是最常见的搭配错误点）
3. **valuable**（有价值的）— 名词 value，动词 value
