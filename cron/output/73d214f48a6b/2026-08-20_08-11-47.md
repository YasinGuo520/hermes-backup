## English Introduction

Anthropic announced that its Claude models successfully designed working protein binders — tiny proteins that latch onto specific targets, which is how many modern drugs work. In one experiment, Claude produced 354 confirmed binders from 1,320 designs, with a success rate between 22 and 35 percent. That is roughly double the 10 to 15 percent success rate that is typical in the field today. The designs were independently tested by two outside companies, Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience, rather than verified in-house. Anthropic says the results are early evidence that AI can dramatically speed up parts of drug development, a process that normally takes human engineers months per target.
