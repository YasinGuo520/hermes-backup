## 📖 English

Nvidia has just released **Kumo Tabular**, an open foundation model that predicts values in a table without any training, tuning, or feature engineering. For two decades, tabular prediction — tasks like churn, default, and demand forecasting — has been dominated by gradient-boosted trees that must be rebuilt from scratch for every new question. Kumo Tabular replaces that workflow with *in-context learning*: you give it a table of labeled rows, and in a single forward pass it returns class probabilities or numeric predictions. The model comes in three sizes, ranging from 28 million to 215 million parameters, and it is available on Hugging Face under a license that allows commercial use.
