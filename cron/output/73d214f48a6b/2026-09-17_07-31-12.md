## 3. 填空练习（2–3 句）

Fill in each blank with the correct form of the verb in brackets.

1. TypeSafe AI claims that Jev ______ (deliver) answers in 70 to 500 milliseconds, which is much faster than a traditional language model.
2. If developers ______ (define) the questions and the allowed answers in advance, the model will simply score each option.
3. The company says the price is low enough that many small judgments in the background ______ (become) economical.

**答案**:
1. **delivers**（一般现在时，表述公司/产品的客观事实）
2. **define**（if 条件句，真实条件用一般现在时表将来）
3. **become / will become**（that 从句表结果，用一般现在时表将来同样正确）

**语法点**：英语里谈"产品的固定性能/客观事实"（如 delivers、costs、charges）一律用一般现在时，不要用 will —— 这是科技新闻里最高频的一条规则。
